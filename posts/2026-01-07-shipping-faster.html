Shipping faster with trunk-based delivery

Jan 7, 2026 · DevOps · 4 min read

Small, frequent changes that are tested and verifiable reduce friction and mean rollback is simple. In this short note I highlight three practical ways to keep your main branch releasable:

1. Keep changes small & reversible

Break work into small PRs or feature flags; prefer toggles and quick rollbacks over long-lived branches.

2. Automate safety gates

Use CI to run unit tests, fast integration tests, and policy checks so the human review focuses on meaning, not on catching regressions.

3. Observe and measure

Track deployment impact with a short feedback loop — error budgets, health checks, and canary releases help you move faster without surprises.

Summary: Small changes + automated gates + rapid feedback = faster, safer shipping.
