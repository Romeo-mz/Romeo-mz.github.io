## Decisions Made

**Rotation range:** Used 40-degree calculation range (x - 0.5) × 40 for -20 to +20 degree rotation, balancing visibility with subtlety per user decision for "moderate 15-20 degree rotation".

**Y-axis inversion:** Implemented (0.5 - y) × 40 for rotateX to create natural tilt behavior (mouse at top tilts card upward, not downward).

**Performance optimization:** Wrapped mousemove calculations in requestAnimationFrame per RESEARCH.md recommendation (lines 203, 577-606) to prevent layout thrashing and maintain 60fps.

**Hover state isolation:** Added isHovering flag to only process mousemove when actively hovering, preventing unnecessary calculations.
