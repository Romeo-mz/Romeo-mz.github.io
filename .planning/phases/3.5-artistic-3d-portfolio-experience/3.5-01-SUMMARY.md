## Decisions Made

**Particle count:** 60 particles chosen from user-specified range (50-80) for balanced visual presence on desktop without performance impact

**Constellation aesthetic:** White star-like points (1-3px radius, rgba 0.8 opacity) evoke stars/nodes in space per user requirement

**Medium energy drift:** Velocity range -0.5 to 0.5 provides noticeable movement without distraction, wrapping around edges for continuous motion

**Connection threshold:** 150px proximity with distance-based fade (`1 - dist/150`) creates dynamic cursor interaction that feels responsive but not overwhelming

**Canvas coordinates:** Used `getBoundingClientRect()` for canvas-relative mouse tracking to ensure robust positioning after window resize (per PLAN.md Task 2 specification)

**Delta time animation:** Multiplier 0.06 on `deltaTime` provides smooth speed calibration for 60fps animation regardless of refresh rate (60Hz vs 120Hz displays)
